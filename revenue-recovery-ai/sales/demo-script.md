# Demo script (under 3 minutes)

## Setup
Have `/demo` open. Have your phone ready to call the demo line.

## 0:00 — Frame it
"I'm going to call your number as if I'm your customer, at 8pm, and you're
asleep. Watch the dashboard while I do it."

## 0:20 — The call
Say: **"My AC isn't cooling and it's 85 degrees inside."**

Let them hear the assistant ask whether it's completely down or running warm.
Give a name and address. Accept a time.

## 1:30 — Show what happened
Refresh the dashboard. Point at, in this order:
1. **The lead** — name, service, urgency, estimated value
2. **The appointment** — a real slot, conflict-checked against their calendar
3. **The conversation** — exactly what it said, word for word
4. **AI activity** — every tool call, logged

"That's a booked job you'd otherwise have found out about tomorrow, if at all."

## 2:15 — Show the guardrail (this closes more than the booking does)
Call back and ask: **"How much for a new system?"**

It will not quote a price. It captures details and escalates.

"That's the part I'd want you to test hardest. It doesn't guess. It would rather
hand you the call than make something up that you then have to honour."

## 2:45 — The ask
"$499 a month. One recovered repair covers it. Want me to set your number up
this week?"

## Do not
- Do not claim a specific revenue number for their business.
- Do not say it replaces their office staff.
- Do not promise it handles every call — it escalates, by design.
