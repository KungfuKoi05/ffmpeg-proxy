# SMS outreach

Only text businesses you have a lawful basis to contact. Check A2P and local
rules first. Every message identifies you and offers an opt-out.

**Initial**
"Hi {{first_name}} — {{sender_name}} here. Quick one: when your team's on a job
and the phone rings, does it go to voicemail? We answer those and book them.
Reply STOP to opt out."

**Follow-up (day 3)**
"{{first_name}} — following up on missed calls. Your number wouldn't change, you
just forward it. Want me to call your line so you can hear it? Reply STOP to opt
out."

**Breakup (day 8)**
"Last one from me — if missed calls become a problem worth fixing, I'm here.
Reply STOP to opt out."
