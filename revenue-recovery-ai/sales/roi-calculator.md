# ROI conversation

## The arithmetic
```
missed calls per month
  × share that could realistically be booked
  × average job value
  = monthly revenue opportunity
```

Only ever call this an **opportunity**. It is not revenue, and not a guarantee.

## Getting honest inputs

**Missed calls.** Most owners underestimate. Their phone system's call log is
the real number. If they have no idea, use their carrier report rather than a
guess.

**Bookable share.** Be conservative — 25–35% is defensible for an assistant that
answers immediately. Do not use 80%.

**Average job value.** Use their repair ticket, not their replacement ticket.
Replacements skew it and make the number look dishonest.

## Worked example
40 missed calls · 30% bookable · $750 average = **$9,000/month opportunity**
against $499/month. One recovered repair covers the subscription.

## What to say
"This is an estimate from your numbers, not a promise. What I'd rather do is run
it for a month and look at the actual booked jobs together. If it doesn't cover
itself, cancel it."

## What not to say
- Any percentage you cannot source.
- "You'll make $X." You do not know that.
- Anything that treats the estimate as booked revenue.
