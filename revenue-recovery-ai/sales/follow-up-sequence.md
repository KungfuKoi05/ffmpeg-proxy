# Follow-up sequence

Post-demo. Stop the moment they say no.

**Day 0 — same day**
Subject: the call you heard

"Thanks for the time. The recording of that call is in your dashboard under
Conversations if you want to replay it. Two things you asked about: [answer 1],
[answer 2]. Want me to set your number up this week?"

**Day 3**
Subject: one number to check

"Rough gut check — how many calls a week do you think go unanswered? Times your
average ticket, that's the number that matters. If it's small, this isn't for
you and I'll say so."

**Day 7**
Subject: after-hours

"The clearest case is nights and weekends. If those already go somewhere useful,
ignore this."

**Day 14 — breakup**
Subject: closing this out

"I'll stop following up. If missed calls become worth fixing, reply here and
I'll pick it back up. Good luck with the season."

## After they sign
- Day 1: confirm the end-to-end test passed.
- Day 3: review anything the assistant escalated; add FAQs for the gaps.
- Day 7: review estimated vs actual revenue; get them entering actual values.
- Day 30: show the month. If it hasn't recovered its cost, say so first.
