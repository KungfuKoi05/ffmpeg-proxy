# Growth — Offer, ICP, Pricing

**Owner:** Growth | **Last updated:** 2026-08-13 | **Status:** ready for founder to send

## Ideal customer profile

**Independent, owner-operated community association management companies managing 15–75 associations,
in FL, AZ, GA, NC, CO, NV, or TX.**

| Attribute | Target |
|---|---|
| Buyer | Owner or principal — someone who can say yes without asking anyone |
| Portfolio | 15–75 associations (≈12–60 board meetings/month) |
| Managers | 2–8 portfolio managers, each carrying 8–12 associations [FACT: https://accuinc.com/how-many-hoas-does-your-community-manager-manage/] |
| Pain trigger | Manager turnover, minutes running late, a board complaining about missing records |
| Disqualifier | National brands with procurement (Associa, FirstService, RealManage, Inframark) |

**Why the management company and not the association:** one HOA generates one meeting a month — a $79
account not worth acquiring. A 30-association firm generates 25–30 meetings a month. Same sales
effort, twenty-five times the revenue. Volunteer boards also decide by committee and turn over
annually; owners decide alone and stay.

## The offer

> **Send us the recording. Get board-ready minutes back in 48 hours.**
>
> Attendance, motions, seconds, votes, and a clean action-item list — formatted to your template,
> ready for the board packet. Your managers stop writing minutes at 9pm.
>
> **The first two meetings are free.** No call required. If you don't want the third, there is
> nothing to cancel.

**Why free first:** on a $0 budget with no case studies and no logo, the only credible asset is the
work itself. A finished document beats any claim we could make about one. It also converts the ask
from "give me 30 minutes of your time" — expensive for a busy owner — to "forward me a file you
already have," which costs them ninety seconds.

## Pricing

| Plan | Price | For |
|---|---|---|
| **Per meeting** | **$79** | Any volume, billed monthly on actual meetings, no minimum, cancel anytime |
| **Portfolio** | **$70/meeting**, 25+ meetings/month | Firms with 25+ associations |
| **Rush** | **+$40** | 24-hour turnaround |
| **Pilot** | **Free** | First two meetings |

**Priced against their cost, not ours.** A portfolio manager spends 2–3 hours per meeting writing
minutes. At a loaded cost of roughly $35/hour that is $70–105 of manager time per meeting
[ESTIMATE: derived from HOA manager salary ranges plus employer burden], for work that produces no
new revenue for the firm. $79 is at or below what they already spend, and it comes back the same week.

**Deliberately not competing with software.** MinuteSmith and BoardBreeze sell tools at $29–699/month
that still require the manager to do the work [FACT: https://minutesmith.com/blog/board-meeting-minutes-software],
[FACT: https://appboardbreeze.com/hoa]. If we price per seat we invite that comparison and lose. We
price per finished document, against labor.

## Guarantee

**Don't like the minutes, don't pay for them.** Per meeting, no argument, no invoice. The founder can
honor this because marginal cost is minutes of QA time — and one refund is cheaper than one bad
review inside a tight-knit industry where owners talk to each other.

## The mathematical model

```
625 prospects → 50 replies (8%) → 25 samples (50%) → 20 proposals (80%) → 8 customers (40%)
8 customers × ~$1,250/mo average = $10,000 MRR
```

At 40 hand-sent touches/week, that is roughly **16 weeks**. Full derivation and downside case:
`analytics/acquisition-model.md`. **Every rate above is `[ASSUMPTION]` until E-001 reads out.** The
close rate is the most suspect number — 40% assumes the free sample does the selling. If it does not,
that is the first thing to fix.

## Channels, in priority order

1. **Hand-sent cold email to named owners** — the primary channel. Free, controllable, measurable.
2. **Referrals** — from customer one onward. CAM owners are densely networked; this should overtake
   cold outreach by rung three.
3. **CAI chapter participation** — local chapters run monthly events. Free to attend, high-trust.
4. **LinkedIn** — follow up with owners who opened but did not reply.
5. **Content** — a genuinely useful minutes template, given away. Slow, compounding, $0.

Paid acquisition stays off the table until unit economics are measured and the founder approves it.

## Expansion path (after 5+ customers)

Same buyer, more revenue per account, no new acquisition cost: action-item tracking between meetings,
violation and architectural review letters (OPP-12), monthly board financial packet assembly
(OPP-14), and annual meeting/election minutes. This is how rung four gets from $5K to $10K without
doubling the logo count.
