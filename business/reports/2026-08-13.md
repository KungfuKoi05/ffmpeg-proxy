## Lessons

**The rubric killed the best problem we found.** Dental insurance verification replaces a role costing
about $3,600/month and sells for $300–800/month — the clearest ROI in the entire study. It needs PHI
handling and the customer's portal credentials, so it was killed. Distinguishing the best problem from
the best problem *for us, right now* was the most valuable thing the scoring system did today.

**The funnel model contradicted the plan before a single email was sent.** The first pass returned 34
weeks to $10K at $1,000/customer. Raising the modeled deal size and narrowing the niche brought it to
16. That correction cost ten minutes on day one instead of four wasted months.

**The economics say the first customer loses money**, at 3% gross margin on 90 minutes of manual work.
That is accepted deliberately, with a written trigger: if delivery is not under 45 minutes by customer
three, the price rises to $120–150 or the business is killed.
