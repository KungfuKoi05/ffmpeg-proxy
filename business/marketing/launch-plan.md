# Launch Plan — $0 Budget

No paid acquisition. No purchased domain. No email infrastructure. Every item is free, and the
sequencing is deliberate: **prove someone replies before building anything they might look at.**

## Phase 0 — Founder setup (before batch 1)

These require a human and cannot be delegated to an agent. All free.

| Task | Cost | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Decide the business name and reply address | $0 | Can be the founder's existing mailbox; a real name and a real signature are all outreach needs |
| Prepare one sample minutes document | $0 | The single most important asset in the entire launch — touch 2 depends on it |
| Read `sales/sequence.md` and `sales/objections.md` | $0 | The founder is the sender; the words have to sound like them |

**A sample from a real meeting is required before batch 1.** Public sources exist — many municipal
and HOA meetings are recorded and posted publicly — and one of those makes an honest, non-confidential
demonstration. Do not fabricate a sample from an imaginary meeting.

## Phase 1 — Validation (weeks 1–3) · the only phase that matters right now

1. Send batch 1: 20 Tier-A prospects from `customers/leads.csv`, touches 1–5 over 18 days
2. Send batch 2: the remaining Tier-A and Tier-B prospects
3. Extend the list to 100 using `customers/lead-sources.md`
4. Log every send, reply, and objection reason in `data/pipeline.json` **the same day**
5. Deliver every requested sample within 24 hours, timing each step

**Success:** ≥1 booked conversation per 10 contacted, or one paid engagement.
**Failure:** the criteria in `DECISION_LOG.md` D-001 — including ≥50% saying their boards do not
record, which kills the model rather than the message.

No landing page is built during this phase. A prospect who wants proof gets the sample document,
which is better than a website.

## Phase 2 — First customer (weeks 3–6)

- Convert a pilot to paid; invoice manually (a Stripe payment link is free to create — **founder
  action**, queued in `APPROVALS.md` when needed)
- Deliver every meeting manually and time each step against the 90-minute budget
- Build the landing page — GitHub Pages, free subdomain — only once someone has asked for a link twice
- Ask the first customer for one referral, by name

## Phase 3 — Repeatability (weeks 6–16)

- Referrals become the primary channel; cold outreach continues at 40/week
- Join the local CAI chapter's free events — this industry buys on relationships
- Publish one genuinely useful free asset: a board minutes template
- Automate only the steps the SOP has proven are stable

## Phase 4 — Paid acquisition (not before $5K MRR)

Only after CAC, margin, and retention are measured, and only with explicit founder approval. Nothing
in this plan depends on it.

## Channels deliberately excluded

**Cold calling** — the founder's 6–10 hours are better spent on personalized email plus delivery.
**Paid ads** — no budget, and no measured economics to justify one.
**Social/organic content** — too slow to matter inside 16 weeks; revisit at Phase 3.
**Marketplaces and lead-gen platforms** — cost money and put a middleman in the relationship.

## What we will not do

No automated bulk sending. No purchased lists. No scraped contacts. No fake urgency, fabricated
testimonials, or invented customer counts. This industry is small and densely networked — one
reported spam complaint or one embarrassing claim would cost more than the entire launch is worth.
