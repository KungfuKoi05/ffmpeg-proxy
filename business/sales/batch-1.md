# Batch 1 — 20 Paste-Ready Emails

Touch 1 of `sequence.md`, instantiated per company. **Nothing here has been sent.**

## Before you start (once, ~2 minutes)

Fill these in your notes and reuse for all 20 — they appear in every signature:

- `[Your name]` — how you sign
- `[phone]` — a number you'll answer
- `[postal address]` — **required by CAN-SPAM** on commercial email. A home address is legal; a
  PO box or virtual mailbox is the usual answer if you'd rather not publish it

## The 30-minute pass

Open `../customers/batch-1-addresses.csv` and work down it with each company's site open:
grab the **owner's name**, the **email**, and — where marked — **one specific detail**. Owner name,
portfolio size, and address all live on the same About or Contact page, so this is one pass, not three.

## Sending rhythm

| Day | Emails | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1–7 | Strongest hooks first — you want early signal on the message itself |
| 2 | 8–14 | |
| 3 | 15–20 | The five needing a detail from you land here, so you have time |

Spread them across the day rather than in one burst. Forty identical sends in ten minutes is what
gets a personal Gmail flagged, and every later touch in the 18-day sequence depends on this account
staying clean.

## After each day

Update `prospects_contacted` in `../data/pipeline.json`, then:

```bash
node tools/kpi.js
```

Log replies as they arrive — **especially the "our boards don't record" ones.** That objection is
experiment E-002, and it can kill the business model on its own. Count it every time it appears.

## When someone replies

`objections.md` covers every objection worth scripting. Three shortcuts:

- **"Send me a sample"** → send it, and ask for one of their recordings in the same message
- **"Sure, here's a recording"** → deliver in **24 hours, not 48**. Beat the promise on the first one
- **"Our boards don't record"** → the two-minute fix is in `objections.md`. **Log it every time** —
  this is E-002, and at ≥50% it kills the model rather than the message

Touches 2 through 5 are in `sequence.md` on days 3, 7, 12, and 18. Most replies come after touch 1,
but a meaningful share won't — send all five before judging the message.
