## What this demonstrates

Worth pointing out explicitly when a prospect reviews it — these are the things that separate real
minutes from an AI transcript summary:

1. **Motions carry exact wording**, with dollar amounts, vendor names, terms, and dates
2. **Vote counts reconcile** to directors present, and dissent is named
3. **The failed motion is recorded** — the entry a transcript-summarizer usually drops
4. **Reports are presenter and topic only** — no summaries padding the document
5. **Executive session is handled to the statutory standard** — occurrence, general subject, action
   taken, nothing confidential
6. **Action items are extracted separately**, with an owner and a date on each
7. **No characterization anywhere** — nothing is "lengthy," "heated," or "productive"

Roughly 500 words for a two-hour meeting. Brevity is the craft, not a shortcut.
