## The rule

**Never ask "would you buy this?"** People are polite, and hypothetical
purchases cost nothing. Ask about what they did last week.

Bad: *"Would an AI follow-up system be useful to you?"*
Good: *"Walk me through the last estimate you sent that didn't close. What
happened after you sent it?"*

## Booking the conversation

Ask for 15 minutes, not 30. Offer something concrete in return: we will tell
them their estimate close rate and where their leads are leaking, whether or not
they ever buy. Best times to reach a trades owner: **7:00–8:00am** or
**4:30–6:00pm**, weekdays. Never mid-morning — they are on a roof or in a truck.

## The script

### Warm-up (2 min)
1. How long have you been running the business, and how many crews do you have now?
2. Where do most of your jobs come from these days?

### Current behaviour — calls (3 min)
3. When the phone rings during a job, who picks it up?
4. What happens to a call nobody catches? *(Listen for: voicemail nobody checks, callback "when I get a minute", nothing.)*
5. Roughly how long before someone gets back to a new caller?
6. Last time you know you lost a job because you were slow — tell me about it.

### Current behaviour — estimates (6 min, THE CORE)
7. How many estimates did you send last month, roughly?
8. What's your average estimate value?
9. Of those, how many closed? *(If they don't know — that's a finding. Note it verbatim.)*
10. **Take the last estimate that didn't close. What happened after you sent it?**
11. Who was supposed to follow up? What did they actually do?
12. How many times do you typically chase before you give up?
13. What happens to an estimate that goes quiet for a month? *(Listen for: nothing. It just sits.)*
14. If a job you'd written off came back three weeks later, what would that be worth?

### Systems and money (3 min)
15. What software holds your estimates and customers?
16. What do you currently pay for leads — LSA, Angi, ads? What's a lead cost you?
17. Have you tried anything to fix follow-up? What happened?
18. Who on the team most hates doing follow-up?

### Close (1 min)
19. If I could show you exactly which estimates went cold and what they were worth, would that be interesting enough for 20 minutes?
20. Who else should I be talking to?

## Signals we are looking for

**Green — proceed:**
- Cannot state their estimate close rate off the top of their head
- Follow-up is "when I remember" or one attempt
- Pays $50+ per lead through LSA or ads (waste is expensive)
- Tells an unprompted story about a job that came back late
- Average estimate value over $5,000

**Red — disqualify or reprice:**
- Already runs a disciplined 4+ touch cadence with a dedicated person
- Average estimate under $2,000 (our fee is hard to justify — say so honestly)
- Books out 3+ months and is turning work away (no felt pain)
- Owner is not the decision maker and won't introduce us
