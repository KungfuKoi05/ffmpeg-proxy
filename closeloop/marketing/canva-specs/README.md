# Canva templates — build specs

**Owner:** Nova · **All on Canva Free.** Nothing here needs Pro.

Brand tokens are in `../brand.md`. Font: **Inter** (free in Canva). Accent
`#0D5C4A`, ink `#14171B`, paper `#FFFFFF`, muted `#4A545F`.

Build them in this order — 1 and 2 are the only ones you need before your first
sales call.

---

## 1. Revenue Leak Audit — the leave-behind ★ build first

**Size:** 1080 × 1920 (A4-ish portrait, exports clean to PDF)
**Used:** attached after every audit call. This is the deliverable that makes
the free audit feel like real work.

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | `Revenue Leak Audit` / business name / date. Accent bar top, lots of white space. |
| 2 | **Their numbers as they gave them.** Six rows: calls/mo, % missed, estimates/mo, avg value, close rate, cost per lead. |
| 3 | **The gap.** One giant accent number: estimated monthly recoverable revenue. Below it, the two-line breakdown (missed calls / unsold estimates). |
| 4 | **The assumptions**, in full, in muted text. Pull straight from the calculator's assumptions panel. *Never hide these.* |
| 5 | **What we'd do** — the 4-touch cadence, drawn as a simple timeline: Day 1, Day 3, Day 7, Day 14. |
| 6 | **What it costs / what happens next.** Price, no contract, review-only start. One CTA. |

Footer on every page, 9pt muted:
> *Figures are estimates based on the numbers you provided and published industry benchmarks. Not a prediction or guarantee.*

---

## 2. Monthly ROI Report ★ build second

**Size:** 1080 × 1920
**Used:** sent to every client monthly. **This is the retention product.**

| Page | Content |
|---|---|
| 1 | Client name, month, one hero figure: revenue attributed. |
| 2 | The funnel: leads → contacted → responded → estimates → won. Simple horizontal bars, accent fill. |
| 3 | **Jobs we're claiming**, itemised: date, customer, amount, and the evidence trail. |
| 4 | **Jobs we're NOT claiming**, itemised with reasons. Muted styling, but present. |
| 5 | Cost vs return. Then: *"Anyone you'd point us at?"* |

> Page 4 is not optional and never gets cut for length. It is the single most
> trust-building page in the whole business.

## 3. Sales one-pager

**Size:** 1080 × 1350 (works as a post and as a print leave-behind)
Headline: *"Your best leads aren't lost. They're just never followed up."*
Then: the 84% stat with its source, three bullets on what we do, the price, one CTA.

## 4. Client onboarding pack

**Size:** 1080 × 1920, 4 pages
What we need from you (the 10 onboarding questions) · what happens in week one ·
the four automation modes explained plainly · who to contact and how fast we respond.

## 5. Case study

**Size:** 1080 × 1350
**Do not build this until a real client has 60 days of attributed data.**
Structure: situation → what was leaking → what we changed → the numbers (real,
attributed) → their words. A blank template is fine to prepare; a filled-in
fictional one is not.

## 9. Lead magnet

**Size:** 1080 × 1920, 6 pages
*"The 4-Touch Estimate Follow-Up System"* — the actual cadence, the actual
message templates, free, no gate beyond an email.

Give away the *what*. We sell the *doing it every day without fail*, which is
the part they won't sustain.

## 10. Monthly performance summary (internal)

**Size:** 1080 × 1080 — MRR, clients, pipeline, biggest bottleneck. For the
founder's own review, one slide, every Friday.

## Production notes

- Build **one** master with the colours and fonts set, then duplicate. Canva Free
  has no Brand Kit — this is the workaround.
- Name files `closeloop-<template>-v<n>`.
- Export PDF Print for leave-behinds, PNG for social.
- Keep a Canva folder per client for their reports.
- Every template carrying a number carries the estimates disclaimer. No exceptions.
