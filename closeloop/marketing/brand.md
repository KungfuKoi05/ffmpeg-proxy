# Brand

**Owner:** Nova

## Name

**Closeloop** — closing the loop on a lead, and closing more of them. Written as
one word, capital C only.

## Positioning

> The follow-up system for contractors whose best leads go quiet.

Not an AI receptionist. Not a chatbot. Not a CRM. A service that works the leads
you already paid for, and proves what it recovered.

## Voice

Write like a competent tradesperson explaining something to another
tradesperson. Plain, specific, unhurried. Never breathless.

**Do:**
- Short sentences. Concrete numbers. Their words, not ours.
- Name the limitation before they find it. *"We don't have case studies yet."*
- Say the price out loud, on the page.

**Don't:**
- "Revolutionary", "game-changing", "10x", "AI-powered", "seamless", "unlock".
- Exclamation marks.
- Fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake social proof, fake logos.
- Any claim we can't substantiate. *"Potential recovered revenue"*, never
  *"we will make you $50,000"*.

**The test:** would you be comfortable if the prospect read this line aloud back
to you in six months, holding their actual results? If not, cut it.

## Colour

| Token | Light | Dark | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accent | `#0D5C4A` | `#4ECFA8` | primary actions, key figures |
| Accent soft | `#E7F3EF` | `#16302A` | badges, highlight panels |
| Ink | `#14171B` | `#E9ECEF` | body text |
| Ink muted | `#4A545F` | `#9AA5B1` | secondary text |
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` | `#15181D` | background |
| Paper alt | `#F5F7F9` | `#1C2026` | alternating bands |
| Line | `#E2E7EC` | `#2C333B` | borders |
| Warning | `#FDF4E3` / `#7A5300` | `#322715` / `#E0B061` | caveats, deferred states |

Deep green: money and steadiness, without the SaaS-purple or the alarm-red that
every competitor in this category uses.

## Type

System font stack throughout — loads instantly, costs nothing, looks native.

```
-apple-system, BlinkMacSystemFont, "Segoe UI", Roboto, Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
```

Headings: 680–720 weight, `-0.02em` tracking. Body: 16px, 1.62 line height.
Numbers in tables: `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`.

For Canva, use **Inter** or **Archivo** — closest free equivalents.

## Logo

Wordmark only. `Close` in ink, `loop` in accent. No icon, no swoosh.

A mark can come later, and only if a client asks for one. Nobody has ever bought
follow-up because of a logo.
