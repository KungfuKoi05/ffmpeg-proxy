# Cold email templates

**Rules:** one verifiable observation per email. Never mass-send. Three touches
then stop. Plain text, no images, no tracking pixels, no HTML signature block.

Everything in `[brackets]` must be replaced with something you actually
observed. If you can't fill a bracket honestly, don't send the email.

---

## Touch 1 — the mystery shop (highest response rate)

**Subject:** `form on [domain].com`

```
[Name] — I filled in the contact form on your site [day] at [time] asking about
[specific job type]. I haven't heard back yet.

I'm not writing to complain — I'm not actually a customer. I run a small
outfit that does follow-up for roofing companies, and I check this before I
reach out to anyone, because it's usually the fastest thing to fix.

Two questions, genuinely curious:

1. Do those form submissions go somewhere someone checks?
2. Of the estimates you sent last month, how many got more than one follow-up?

If the answer to the second one is "not many", that's normal — surveys put
same-day follow-up on unsold estimates at about 16% of roofers. It's also where
most of the money is.

Worth 15 minutes?

[Your name]
[phone]
```

**If they responded quickly, don't send this.** Use Touch 1B.

---

## Touch 1B — for businesses that DO respond fast

**Subject:** `you answered in [X] minutes`

```
[Name] — I submitted your form on [day] as a test and got a reply in [X]
minutes. That puts you ahead of almost everyone in your trade, so this probably
isn't for you.

The one place I'd still ask about: the estimates that don't close. When you
send a number and hear nothing, what happens next?

Most shops that are great on the phone still let quotes go quiet, because
follow-up on a $12k estimate is nobody's job specifically. If that sounds
familiar, I'd like 15 minutes. If you've got that covered too, tell me to get
lost and I'll leave you alone.

[Your name]
```

## Touch 2 — the specific number (3–4 days later)

**Subject:** `re: form on [domain].com`

```
[Name] — following up once.

If you send roughly [N] estimates a month at around [$X] each and close about a
quarter of them, there's somewhere near [$Y] of quoted work a month that goes
quiet. Not lost to a competitor on price — just never chased.

We work those, in your voice, and you approve every message before it sends.
At the end of the month you get a report showing which jobs came back, with the
message and the customer's reply attached to each one.

15 minutes this week? Wednesday or Thursday morning works well.

[Your name]
```

---

## Touch 3 — the close-out (5–7 days later)

**Subject:** `closing your file`

```
[Name] — I'll stop here.

If follow-up on unsold estimates ever becomes the thing you want to fix, I'm at
[phone]. I'll keep an eye on [business] either way.

[Your name]
```

*Then actually stop.* Revisit in 90 days if the signals still look good.

---

## Referral ask (after a real result, never before)

**Subject:** `who else should I talk to?`

```
[Name] — [month]'s report is attached. [N] jobs traced back to follow-up,
[$X] total.

One ask: is there anyone in [city] you'd point me at? Not a list — one name of
someone whose work you respect. Happy to do the same for you.

[Your name]
```
