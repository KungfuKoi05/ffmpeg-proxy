    Straight answers

    Isn't this just a missed-call text-back tool?

    Those exist and cost $50 to $200 a month. If missed-call text-back is all you need, buy one of those - we'll even name a few. What they don't do is work your unsold estimates, and that's where the $14,000 jobs are sitting. That's what we're built for.

    Will this text my customers without me knowing?

    No. Every account starts in review-only mode, where messages are written and logged but never sent. The next mode requires you to approve each message before it goes out. Automatic sending is a deliberate switch you flip, and you can flip it back in one click.

    How do I know the results are real?

    Every job we claim shows the message we sent, the customer's reply, and the date it closed. If we can't trace that chain, we don't claim it - and the report shows those jobs too, in a separate column, so you can see what we left on the table.

    What about consent and STOP requests?

    We don't send marketing texts to anyone without a consent record, STOP is honoured immediately and permanently, and every marketing sequence carries an opt-out in its first message. Quiet hours are enforced in your local timezone.

    Do I have to replace my CRM?

    No. We sit alongside whatever you use. If you want the data back in your system, we'll wire that up.

    What if it doesn't work?

    Cancel with 30 days' notice. If after 60 days we haven't produced traceable revenue worth more than what you paid, we'd rather you left than kept paying for something that isn't working.
