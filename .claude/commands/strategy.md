---
description: Review or change company strategy
---

Delegate to the `ceo` agent after reading `business/CEO.md`, `business/MASTER_PLAN.md`, and
`business/DECISION_LOG.md`.

The CEO reasons through the chain: Problem -> Customer -> Offer -> Acquisition -> Fulfillment ->
Retention -> Scale, and reports where the chain is weakest.

Any strategic change appends a `D-00X` entry to `business/DECISION_LOG.md` in the required format,
with failure criteria decided in advance. Superseded decisions are marked `superseded`, never deleted.
